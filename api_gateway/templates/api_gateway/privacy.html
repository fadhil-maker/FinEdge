Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2023

1. Our Zero-Knowledge Promise

At FinEdge, we believe that financial inclusion should never come at the cost of personal privacy. We have engineered our platform from the ground up to operate on a "zero-knowledge" principle.

When you apply for a loan through one of our Partner Banks, our Edge SDK analyzes your smartphone footprint entirely on-device. We do not transmit your raw SMS data, your contact names, or your personal photos to any external server.

2. DPDP Act, 2023 Compliance

We are fully compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). Our architecture ensures:

  • Explicit Consent: No extraction occurs without explicit, granular user consent.
  • Data Minimization: We extract only 7 highly abstracted behavioral features (e.g., utility payment frequency, device age).
  • Zero Retention: After the local extraction process completes, the raw data used for analysis is immediately and permanently purged from the local RAM.

3. Data Security & Cryptography

Once the mathematical feature vector is generated on your device, it is cryptographically signed using an HMAC-SHA256 hash. This guarantees that the payload cannot be tampered with in transit. The transmission to our secure Django backend occurs over TLS 1.3 encrypted channels.

4. Anti-Fraud Measures

To protect both users and our partner banks, we generate a one-way anonymous hash of your device to detect "velocity" fraud (e.g., applying for dozens of loans across different banks simultaneously). This hash cannot be reverse-engineered to identify your physical device serial numbers.
