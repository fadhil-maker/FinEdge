The Problem We Solve

Traditional credit bureaus rely on historical loan data, leaving millions outside the formal financial system.

300M+

Unbanked Indians

Lack formal credit history to access mainstream financial products.

₹3.5T

Addressable Market

Micro-lending opportunity unlocked through alternative scoring.

80%

Rejection Rate

For thin-file borrowers using traditional underwriting methods.

FinEdge bridges this gap by turning the borrower's smartphone into a secure assessment tool. By analyzing alternative data sources—like device metadata, utility SMS counts, and behavioral patterns—we generate a reliable trust score. Crucially, all feature extraction happens on the device. Only an anonymized mathematical vector is sent to our servers, ensuring total privacy.

Enterprise-Grade Features

Built for scale, security, and compliance from day one.

On-Device Feature Extraction

Edge SDK collects behavioral & transactional signals locally on the user's smartphone without sending raw data over the network.

Privacy-First Architecture

Raw SMS, contacts, and app data never leave the device. Compliant with the strictest data protection laws including the DPDP Act 2023.

XGBoost ML Scoring

Production-ready ML model assessing risk using a 7-feature mathematical vector, trained on thousands of diverse borrower profiles.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Feature-level impact analysis for every credit decision. Know exactly why a score was assigned with positive and negative signal breakdowns.

Multi-Tenant B2B SaaS

Tenant isolation, secure API key authentication, and HMAC signature verification ensure your institution's data remains segregated and safe.

Usage-Based Billing

Transparent ₹10 per API call metered billing. Pay only for the assessments you process, complete with detailed audit trails and reporting.
